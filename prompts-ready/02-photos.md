## Сообщение 0 — мир и стиль (без генерации)
```
We are going to create a series of photographs for one luxury brand. Please remember this world and style for every image in this project.

WORLD: Red Fox, a luxury restaurant on the snowy summit of Hollister Peak, a high alpine ski resort, on New Year's Eve. The restaurant is an alpine stone lodge: walls of dark grey rough-cut stone, a steep snow-covered gabled roof with dark timber eaves and a stone chimney, a large arched multi-pane window in a carved stone surround with frost in its corners, smaller rectangular side windows, warm brass lantern sconces, and elegant warm backlit serif letters "RED FOX" on the gable. A small gondola station stands beside it; far below lie the warm lights of a ski village. Inside: a grand dining hall with dark wooden floors, timber-beamed ceiling, brass candle chandeliers, a massive stone fireplace, a tall Christmas tree with warm lights and red and gold ornaments, round tables with white linen, candles and spruce garlands, and a long backlit amber onyx bar.
PEOPLE: elegant guests in black tie and evening gowns — velvet, silk, sequins — aged 30 to 65, diverse. Waiters wear dark navy-black waistcoats over crisp white long-sleeved shirts, black bow ties and white gloves. Bartenders wear black waistcoats with rolled-up white sleeves.
STYLE for every image: cinematic luxury editorial photography, large-format cinema camera, shallow depth of field, subtle Kodak Vision3 500T film grain. Lit only by light sources inside the scene — candles, chandeliers, garland lights, fireplace, lanterns, moonlight on snow. Warm amber highlights, deep blue-ink shadows, rich blacks, nothing flat or overexposed. Quiet, intimate, expensive. Photorealistic. 16:9 unless I say otherwise.
AVOID in every image: any text except "RED FOX" when I ask for it, watermarks, logos, cartoon or CGI look, plastic skin, distorted hands, extra fingers, oversaturated colours, daylight.

Reply "ready" and wait for my first image.
```
